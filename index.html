Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)

BNS Section 63 defines rape, and Section 64 covers punishment for rape. The BNS came into effect on 1 July 2024. Older cases may involve the former IPC provisions.

Official India Code ↗
🛡

POCSO Act, 2012

The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act protects people under 18 from sexual offences. It provides child-sensitive procedures and reporting safeguards.

Official India Code ↗
📄

Zero FIR

A Zero FIR can be registered at a police station regardless of where the offence occurred, then transferred to the appropriate police station. You do not have to know the correct jurisdiction first.

Ministry of Home Affairs ↗

What does consent mean?

Consent means a freely given, clear and voluntary agreement. Silence, fear, pressure or lack of resistance is not consent. A person can change their mind. Being in a relationship or having consented before does not mean consent now.

Practical guidance

How to report an incident

The process can feel difficult. You can ask a trusted person or a support professional to help you understand your options.

1

Prioritise immediate safety

Move to a safer place if possible. Call 112 if you face immediate danger. Consider contacting a trusted person.

2

Contact the police

You can approach a police station to report the incident. A Zero FIR may be registered irrespective of jurisdiction. Ask for assistance if communication or accessibility is difficult.

3

Ask about your rights and options

You may ask about recording your statement, privacy safeguards, a copy of the FIR and access to a support person or legal aid where applicable.

4

Seek medical and emotional support

A hospital or One Stop Centre may help connect you to medical care, counselling and other services. You can ask about available options even if you are unsure what to do next.

Rights and protections to ask about

✓ Privacy and identity protection under applicable law
✓ Access to free legal aid, subject to applicable provisions
✓ Medical care and information about support services
✓ Information about the complaint and investigation process
✓ Child-sensitive procedures for cases covered by POCSO
✓ Assistance from a support person or interpreter, where available

Exact procedures and entitlements depend on the circumstances and applicable law. A lawyer or legal services authority can explain them.

Free legal aid — NALSA

Women and children are among the categories eligible for free legal services under Indian law. Contact NALSA or your District Legal Services Authority to understand eligibility and assistance.
